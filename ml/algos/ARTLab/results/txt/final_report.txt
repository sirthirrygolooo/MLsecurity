=== Adversarial Attack Benchmark Report ===

Key Findings:
- The model's accuracy drops from 86.94% to 64.59% under FGSM attack (25.71% reduction)
- Under PGD attack, accuracy drops to 61.47%
- Under DeepFool attack, accuracy drops to 13.08%
- DeepFool typically finds more efficient perturbations than FGSM/PGD
- The average inference time increases significantly under attacks, with DeepFool being the most computationally intensive
